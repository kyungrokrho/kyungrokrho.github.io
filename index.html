Research & Projects

First author · Under review at ICRA 2027

CarryMimic

Stable whole-body object carrying with a humanoid robot.

I designed a force-closure reward that encourages grasps capable of withstanding the forces and torques of a carrying motion. I developed and evaluated the method in simulation and ran the hardware experiments on a Unitree G1.

Vision-language manipulation

LIMBO

Robot manipulation from natural-language instructions.

I worked on the manipulation module and applied the same vision-language approach to a robotic arm and a humanoid arm. The demos included picking up a hammer and placing an apple to the left of a banana.
